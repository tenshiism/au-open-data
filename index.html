Victoria is warmingSAMPLE DATA

Source: Bureau of Meteorology ACORN-SAT · Victoria annual mean temperature anomaly vs 1961–1990

Each stripe is one year, coloured by how far Victoria's mean temperature sat above (red) or below (blue) the 1961–1990 average. Hover a stripe for the value.

Rents vs wagesSAMPLE DATA

Source: ABS CPI rents (8 capital cities) · ABS Wage Price Index (Australia) · seasonally adjusted, 2005 = 100

Both series are indexed to the same base year so they share one axis: when the rent line pulls above the wage line, renting is getting less affordable.
